# Recipe Index

Welcome to our Minecraft recipe reference.

This vault documents the recipes and production chains used in our current world, including the machines required, input materials, outputs, probabilities, and production workflows.

> [!info]  
> Recipes are documented based on the machines and mods available in **our current world**. They may differ from recipes in other modpacks, versions, or worlds.

## Production Chains

Some items aren't produced directly. Instead, they form part of a longer production chain.

For example:

**Gold Ingot → [[Gold Plate]] → [[Small Cogwheel]]s → [[Precision Mechanism]]**

The individual recipe pages document these dependencies and show the production flow using diagrams.

## Recipe Format

Each recipe page contains:

- **Inputs** — materials consumed by the recipe
    
- **Requirements** — machines needed to perform the recipe
    
- **Outputs** — expected products and probabilities
    
- **Workflow Diagram** — a visual representation of the production process
